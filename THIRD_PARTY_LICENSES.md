# Third-Party Rules and Trademark Register

This is a production checklist, not legal advice. Recheck controlling terms before every commercial release.

## D&D 2014
- Publishing baseline: SRD 5.1 under CC BY 4.0.
- Exclude non-SRD books, settings, characters, art, maps, logos, and trade dress.

## D&D 2024
- Publishing baseline: SRD 5.2.1 under CC BY 4.0.
- Do not use D&D Beyond Basic Rules as the publishing source.
- Keep 2024 and 2014 content edition-labeled.

## Pathfinder Second Edition Remaster
- Use only material designated Licensed Material under ORC.
- ORC excludes Paizo trademarks, setting content, named characters, art, maps, logos, and trade dress.
- The Paizo Compatibility License is separate and governs specified trademark/logo use.

## Call of Cthulhu Seventh Edition
- No general open Call of Cthulhu rules license.
- Website content must remain original educational commentary and official links.
- Miskatonic Repository is the community-content route for qualifying commercial material.
- BRP Universal Game Engine is available under ORC, but its license reserves the Call of Cthulhu trademark and other product identity.

## Daggerheart
- Use Public Game Content from Daggerheart SRD 1.0 under the current Darrington Press Community Gaming License.
- Follow current attribution, logo, format, name-mark, and prohibited-content rules.

## Vampire: The Masquerade Fifth Edition
- No general open tabletop SRD.
- Dark Pack primarily covers qualifying noncommercial fan activity.
- Storytellers Vault covers qualifying commercial tabletop books.
- Unbound is a separate V5 video-game program for itch.io and is not a general website or tabletop license.

## Repository rules
1. Use original prose unless exact licensed text is necessary.
2. Record source, license version, attribution, edition, and review date.
3. Use original art and visual identity.
4. Never imply endorsement or official status.
5. Audit text, names, art, metadata, screenshots, downloads, and advertising before release.
